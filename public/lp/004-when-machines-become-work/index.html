05 / THE SYSTEM AROUND THE ROBOT

The robot is visible. The system carries the economics.

Tooling, sensing, safety, integration, and service turn one visible action into an operating system. Its uptime, utilization, and deployment burden shape the cost of useful work—and the capital required to scale.

A lower hardware price does not automatically establish a lower cost per useful task.

06 / WHAT CHANGES THE MATH

Capacity is not output until useful time accumulates.

Ask how often the system runs, what interrupts it, and which costs arrive before utilization. Then test whether greater deployment could improve—or strain—operating leverage.
